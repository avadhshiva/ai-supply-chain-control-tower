# AI Supply Chain Control Tower

> Agentic AI-powered Executive Intelligence Platform for Supply Chain Operations

An enterprise-inspired AI platform that transforms operational supply chain data into executive-ready insights, intelligent recommendations, and decision support using Large Language Models (LLMs).

Rather than functioning as a traditional analytics dashboard, the platform demonstrates how Agentic AI can assist Supply Chain, Operations, Logistics, and Executive Leadership by identifying risks, summarizing operational health, and recommending prioritized actions.

---

# Vision

Supply chain leaders often spend significant time gathering reports from multiple systems before making operational decisions.

The objective of this project is to demonstrate how AI can become an intelligent decision-support partner by:

- Consolidating operational metrics
- Identifying business risks
- Prioritizing critical issues
- Generating executive summaries
- Providing AI-assisted recommendations
